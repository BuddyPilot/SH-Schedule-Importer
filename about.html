SH → Sked

SH Sked turns a monthly crew scheduling workbook into events on your own Google Calendar. It was built for 737 flight instructors who otherwise copy each month's assignments across by hand.

What it does

  • Reads the monthly schedule spreadsheet and finds your line on it.
  • Shows every event it found so you can check it against the sheet before anything is written.
  • Writes those events to a calendar you choose, with the report time, room number, and reminders already set.
  • Re-running next month updates the existing entries rather than creating duplicates.

How it works

Everything happens in your browser. The spreadsheet is read on your own device and never uploaded anywhere. There is no server, no database, and no account to create.

When you connect your Google account, the app receives a temporary access token that lives in the browser tab and is discarded when you close it. Nothing about your Google account is stored.

What it asks for

  • See and edit events on your calendars — used to create and update your schedule entries.
  • See a list of your calendars — used only so you can choose which calendar receives the events.

Nothing else is requested, and nothing is read from your calendar beyond the list of calendar names.
